2. Description of Service

HortiWise is a plant care companion application for iOS and Android devices that allows users to:

  • Create and manage plant profiles with care schedules and reminders
  • Capture and store plant photos locally on the device
  • Identify plants and run health checks using AI-powered visual analysis
  • Receive weather-based watering and care suggestions
  • Use voice commands to interact with garden features (where available)
  • Keep a garden journal with notes and photos
  • Share plant achievements via the system share sheet
  • Subscribe to premium features through in-app purchases

HortiWise does not operate backend servers to store your garden data. Most features work with data stored locally on your device.
