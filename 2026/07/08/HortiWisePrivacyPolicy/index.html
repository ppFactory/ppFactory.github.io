HortiWisePrivacyPolicy

Email

Email: vomiv523@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how HortiWise (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use our plant care companion application for iOS and Android (“App”). HortiWise is designed to help you track plants, receive care reminders, and get AI-assisted identification and health insights.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what data stays on your device, what is sent to third-party services, and what we do not collect on our own servers.

2. Our Privacy Principles

HortiWise is built with a local-first, no-backend approach:

  • No proprietary servers: We do not operate backend servers to store or aggregate your garden data.
  • No user accounts: We do not require registration, login, or a centralized user profile.
  • Your garden data stays on your device: Plant profiles, journals, photos, and care plans are stored locally unless you explicitly trigger a feature that requires internet access.
  • User-initiated AI only: Plant photos are sent for analysis only when you tap Identify or Health Check.
  • Location for weather only: Location, postal code, or IP-based approximation is used solely to fetch weather forecasts and watering suggestions — not for background tracking or advertising targeting.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect on Our Servers

Because HortiWise has no backend infrastructure, we do not:

  • Operate servers that store your plant photos, journals, or garden records
  • Maintain a cloud backup or sync service for your garden data
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal identifiers (unless you voluntarily contact support)
  • Collect or store payment card or banking information (purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google)
  • Track your precise home address in the background

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Plant profiles: Names, types, environment settings, care schedules, and watering history
  • Garden journal entries: Notes, health check results, and optional journal photos
  • Plant photos: Saved in your device’s local app storage (not on HortiWise servers)
  • Location preferences: City, region, country, postal code, and approximate coordinates used for weather (cached locally for convenience)
  • Care plans and task recommendations: Generated care schedules and feedback
  • App settings: Notification preferences, unit settings (metric/imperial), onboarding status
  • Subscription entitlement cache: A local record of your premium status (no payment details)

This data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the App, or reset your device.

5. AI Analysis and Photos

5.1 Local Photo Storage

Plant photos you capture or select are stored locally on your device. HortiWise does not upload photos to our own servers.

5.2 When Photos Are Sent to AI

Photos are transmitted only when you actively request AI features:

  • Plant Identification: When you tap Identify
  • Health Check: When you tap Health Check and submit a photo for analysis

In these cases, your photo is temporarily sent over a secure connection to Alibaba DashScope (Qwen VL models) for processing. Analysis results are returned to your device and stored locally.

Photos are not sent for identification or health analysis unless you tap the relevant action. This is also shown in the App: “Photos are sent for identification only when you tap Identify.”

5.3 What AI Processes

The AI analysis:

  • Processes only the plant images you choose to submit
  • Provides identification suggestions, health scores, and care recommendations
  • Does not give DashScope or HortiWise ongoing access to your entire photo library
  • Is performed through secure API calls; we do not permanently store your photos on HortiWise servers

Retention of data processed by DashScope is governed by Alibaba Cloud’s policies.

6. Location and Weather

HortiWise uses location-related information only to provide weather-based care suggestions:

  • GPS coordinates (when you grant location permission)
  • Postal code (when you enter it manually)
  • IP-based approximate location (when no GPS or postal code is set, via weather providers)

This information is sent to WeatherAPI.com and/or QWeather (Hefeng) to retrieve forecasts. We do not use location for background tracking, advertising targeting, or building location profiles.

A summary of your location preference (city, region, approximate coordinates) may be cached locally on your device for faster weather updates.

7. Voice Features

HortiWise includes voice interaction for garden commands:

  • On-device speech recognition: When available, speech-to-text runs on your device using Apple’s or Google’s speech recognition services.
  • Cloud AI routing (when enabled): If you use voice commands that require AI interpretation, your transcribed text (not raw audio stored by us) and relevant garden context (such as plant names) may be sent to DashScope to determine your intent.

We do not operate servers to store your voice recordings.

9. Advertising

Free users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob, including rewarded video ads and app-open ads. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers and interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google’s policies.

On iOS, HortiWise may participate in Apple’s SKAdNetwork for ad attribution. You can limit ad tracking through your device settings (e.g., “Limit Ad Tracking” on iOS or advertising settings on Android).

10. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used and improve the App. Events may include, for example:

  • Onboarding completion
  • Paywall views and purchase events
  • Plant identification and health check usage
  • Home screen interactions

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and fatal errors so we can fix stability issues. Crash reports may include device model, OS version, and stack traces.

These analytics are processed by Google Firebase, not stored on HortiWise servers. You can learn more at Firebase Privacy.

11. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

Permission Why We Ask
Camera Capture plant photos for identification, health checks, and journals
Photo Library Select existing plant photos
Location (while in use) Fetch local weather for watering and care suggestions
Microphone Voice commands on the voice assistant screen
Speech Recognition Convert speech to text on your device
Notifications Local care reminders and task alerts

Permission descriptions shown in the App match the use cases above.

12. Children’s Privacy

HortiWise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us at vomiv523@gmail.com.

13. Data Retention

13.1 Local Data

Your garden data remains on your device until you:

  • Delete individual plants or journal entries
  • Uninstall the App
  • Reset or replace your device

13.2 Third-Party Processing

  • DashScope: Processes photos and text temporarily for AI features; retention is subject to Alibaba Cloud’s policies.
  • Weather providers: Process location queries to return forecasts; we do not control their retention periods.
  • Firebase / AdMob: Retain analytics, crash, and ad data per Google’s policies.

We do not maintain long-term copies of your garden content on HortiWise servers.

14. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your region, you may have rights regarding your personal information. Because HortiWise stores most data locally:

  • Access and deletion: You can view and delete plants, journals, and photos within the App, or remove all App data by uninstalling.
  • Location: You can deny location permission and enter a postal code instead, or clear location settings in the App.
  • AI features: You control when photos are sent by choosing whether to tap Identify or Health Check.
  • Analytics and ads: You may limit ad tracking and analytics through your device or platform settings.
  • Contact us: For privacy requests, email vomiv523@gmail.com.

If you are in the European Economic Area or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

15. International Data Transfers

When you use AI or weather features, your data may be processed in countries other than your own. DashScope servers may be located in jurisdictions such as China, Singapore, or the United States. We use secure transmission (HTTPS) for all network requests.

16. Security

HortiWise implements:

  • Local storage for garden data and photos
  • Secure HTTPS communication with third-party APIs
  • API keys stored in secure device storage, not hard-coded in the App binary

Your data security also depends on your device settings (passcode, biometrics, OS updates, and backup practices).

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

18. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: vomiv523@gmail.com

Your privacy is our priority. HortiWise keeps your garden on your device, uses location only for weather, and sends photos to AI only when you ask.
