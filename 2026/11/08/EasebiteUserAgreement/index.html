2. Description of Service

Easebite is a meal companion for iOS and Android. You can:

  • Photograph a meal, or choose a photo from your library
  • See foods the photo appears to show, and one small adjustment that may be easier on your stomach
  • Save what tends to bother you, foods that are usually fine, food allergies, and an optional bedtime
  • Note how you felt after a meal
  • Use an on-device eating rhythm while you chew
  • Receive local reminders, if you turn them on
  • Subscribe to continue without a rewarded ad

Easebite does not operate servers that store your meals. Photos, feelings, and your profile stay on your device, except for the requests described in our Privacy Policy.

3. Not Medical Advice

Easebite is an informational companion. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Meal suggestions, food names, and patterns drawn from your after-meal notes:

  • Can be incomplete or wrong
  • Are not a reason to start, stop, or change medication, or to ignore symptoms
  • Do not replace a doctor, dietitian, or allergist

Allergies. A photo may not show every ingredient, sauce, or point of cross-contact. Removing a visible piece does not make the rest of the meal safe. Check the package or ask the restaurant. If you have a food allergy, do not rely on the App to decide whether a meal is safe to eat.

Symptoms. Reflux, bloating, pain, nausea, burning, and early fullness can have causes the App cannot see. Seek medical care for severe, persistent, or worsening symptoms, and for any emergency.

Eating rhythm. One circle is one bite and one step is one chew. You can stop at any time. The rhythm is not a chewing quota, not a score, and not a judgment of how you ate.

5.2 Services That Need the Internet

Some features need a connection:

  • Repeated-food comparison: After you save feelings, if one food appears in two rated meals, food names and those yes-or-no answers may be sent as text. The photo is not included.
  • Ads, crashes, and purchases: Google AdMob, Firebase, and Apple or Google may process the data described in the Privacy Policy.

Please read the Privacy Policy.

6. AI Features

Meal recognition and the small adjustment use Alibaba DashScope. This:

  • Needs an internet connection
  • Runs only for a meal you choose to analyze
  • Returns results to your device
  • Does not store the photo on Easebite servers

The eating-rhythm camera does not use DashScope. Chew detection runs on the device.

Results are suggestions about what the photo appears to show. They can miss foods, misname them, or propose an adjustment that does not fit you. Use your own judgment.
